## Persona 1: Sai Ganesh

**Age:** 23

**Job:** Junior Developer at a startup

**Background:** Sai Ganesh just finished college. He works on Python and Django daily. He gets stuck often and uses StackOverflow to find solutions. He wants to learn and also help others when he can.

**What he wants:**
- Find code solutions quickly
- Know which answers are correct (by votes)
- Ask questions when stuck
- Follow experts to see their answers

**What bothers him:**
- Too many wrong or outdated answers
- Gets downvoted for simple questions
- Cannot find experts in his tech stack

**Quote:** *"I tried everything I could. I need help to move forward."*
